# Contributing

risk-rs is open source under your choice of the
[MIT](LICENSE-MIT) or [Apache-2.0](LICENSE-APACHE) licence, and is owned and
directed by Ethan Kang.

- **Contributor licence agreement.** Before a pull request can merge, its
  author signs the [CLA](CLA.md) (one comment on the PR; the CLA Assistant
  check records it). You keep your copyright; the CLA lets the project
  keep distributing your work and change its licence if ever needed.
- **Direction.** The maintainer decides what goes in. Open an issue to
  discuss a new feature before you write it; the plan is in
  [docs/roadmap.md](docs/roadmap.md) and [docs/architecture.md](docs/architecture.md).
- **How to work.** [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) describes the layout, the checks
  to run before you push (`cargo fmt`, `cargo clippy`, `cargo test`, and
  the Python and R checks when you touch the bindings), and the knowledge
  bundle in `knowledge/`.
- **Licence of contributions.** Unless you state otherwise, a contribution
  is licensed as the project is (MIT OR Apache-2.0), with the additional
  rights in the CLA.
