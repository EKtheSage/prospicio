# Findings

* [Evaluating a reinsurance submission with prospicio, what the API covers](program-evaluation-api-map.md) - Each step from on-level premium to monitoring mapped onto the Python API of main: what ran, caveats (ages in months, on_grid refuses pro_rata_time, silent wild alphas from match_tower, a Tower.apply join double counts 18.68m against 10.09m) the gaps, issues 190 to 197, and what an end-to-end run added (a policy-limit mix has no object, `Pareto.fit` has no standard error, development measured on a listing is selected on the outcome, stand-alone TVaR capital against one price of risk for a top layer).
* [Automatic differentiation for small Bayesian reserving models, benchmarked](autodiff-benchmark.md) - On Meyers CRC (29 parameters) every option matched the hand gradient to 1e-15; an in-house dual cost 4 to 5 times it, num-dual 10 to 90, candle about 100, Burn about 190; Burn 0.21 sigmoid rounds to f32 on f64.
* [Evaluating a reinsurance submission with prospicio, what the API covers](program-evaluation-api-map.md) - Each step from on-level premium to monitoring mapped onto the Python API of main: what ran, caveats (ages in months, on_grid refuses pro_rata_time, silent wild alphas from match_tower, a Tower.apply join double counts 18.68m against 10.09m) and the gaps, issues 190 to 197.
* [Third raw moments, closed forms against mpmath](third-moments.md) - Severity and Counting third moments match mpmath at 30 digits to 1e-10; mpmath's nsum fails on a Poisson mean of 1,000; the mixed Poisson's pmf carries about 2e-12 into its moment.
* [Normal draws bias the log-link mean](odp-normal-draws-bias.md) - Drawing beta from N(beta_hat, Sigma) and exponentiating raises each mean by exp(x'Sigma x / 2); MeanPreserving draws remove it exactly.
* [Negative increments in the ODP](odp-negative-increments.md) - A quasi-likelihood ODP needs only V(mu) = mu and mu > 0; negative responses are fitted with a quasi-deviance that is not a distance.
* [ODP log density](odp-continuous-density.md) - The ODP's lattice probability is -inf off the lattice; its continuation through Gamma, normalized by C(lambda), is a proper density in y.
* [Stacking pooling funnel](stacking-pooling-funnel.md) - With one slope per pooled group, the group scale and the slope trade off; NUTS diverges. Three or more covariates mix well.
* [R callbacks must stay on R's main thread](r-callbacks-main-thread.md) - An R function held inside a Rust object may be called only from R's thread; parallel code that meets one runs in order on the calling thread.
* [Clash and the bivariate compound FFT against aggregate](clash-bivariate-parity.md) - aggregate's clash fixes the events hitting neither line by independence (n0 = na nb / nc), so a Poisson count gives Cov(A, B) = nc E[X] E[Y]; the 2-D FFT matches aggregate 1.0.1 at 1e-12 once its 1e-15 fuzz clip, which drops about 1e-11 of tail mass and moves second moments by 3e-9, is off.
* [Renewal claim counts against aggregate](renewal-count-parity.md) - The Plancherel renewal count matches aggregate 1.0.1 at 1e-8 on its own wait grid and is closer to the exact values (5e-11 against 6e-9 at k = 0 and 1); one packed complex FFT for the tilted wait and indicator fails, the indicator being up to e^15 times the wait.
* [Wiener-Hopf ruin for renewal claims against aggregate](wiener-hopf-parity.md) - The cepstral Wiener-Hopf ruin matches aggregate 1.0.1 at 3e-12 once the severity is discretized aggregate's way; Grid::rounding's tail on the last point moves a lognormal's mean by 1e-9 and psi by 2e-9.
* [Reinstatements pro rata as to time](reinstatement-pro-rata-time.md) - Each event's used limit is charged at the share of the year left after it; sorted uniform (or seasonal) times in drawn order date i.i.d. losses exactly; one exhausting loss a year costs half the amount-only premium.
* [Bondy least squares](bondy-least-squares-stop.md) - TailBondy fits b with scipy least_squares at default tolerances, which stop on a 1e-8 relative cost change; b is off by up to 4e-6, the tail's results by up to 3e-5.
* [The simulated one-year view against Merz-Wuthrich, ODP and Mack's process](one-year-bootstrap-vs-merz-wuthrich.md) - Re-reserving on the ODP bootstrap gives 0.50 to 5.96 times Merz-Wuthrich's one-year SD per origin; under Mack's process (MackBootstrap) the same re-reserving reconciles its standard deviation with Merz-Wuthrich within Monte Carlo error on RAA, GenIns and ABC and matches EVW (2019) Table 4, so the gap is the ODP's process model; its mean CDR is biased unless the residuals are centred, the default. A quarterly split of an annual triangle gets about half its one-year SD.
* [Mack's bootstrap, lifetime view, against Mack's standard errors and EVW Table 4](mack-bootstrap-lifetime-vs-mack.md) - MackBootstrap::fit reproduces Mack's standard error on RAA, GenIns and ABC only with centred residuals, its parameter error scaled by the residuals' variance 1 - m^2; uncentred, the mean reserve is biased (RAA +17%); EVW's Table 4 expected reserves agree with centred residuals, the default.
* [Stream scheme and samplers, two records in a result's provenance](provenance-samplers.md) - Seeded results record the sampler versions (gamma = marsaglia-tsang/2026-10) apart from the stream scheme; join's seed check compares seed and scheme, replay also samplers; results saved before 2026-10-08 carry no samplers.
* [The Gamma sampler, Marsaglia-Tsang instead of inverse transform](gamma-sampler.md) - Gamma::sample draws by Marsaglia and Tsang (2000) on the same streams: 0.13 to 0.22 microseconds a draw at any shape against up to 375 by bisecting the cdf; every Gamma draw changed and is no longer monotone in one uniform.
* [Dependence between reserving lines, synchronized bootstrap and rank correlation](synchronized-bootstrap-correlation.md) - Resampling the same residual positions in every line gives the lines' parameter error about their residuals' correlation (ODP within 0.055, Mack within 0.145 on the CLRD lines), diluted by process error; Iman-Conover reproduces a Spearman matrix converted to 2 sin(pi rho / 6); one seed's correlations are shared across pairs.
* [The inverse Gaussian without e^(2λ/μ)](inverse-gaussian-mills-ratio.md) - With the Mills ratio R, F = Φ(a) + φ(a) R(b) and S = φ(a)(R(a) - R(b)), exactly, so nothing overflows at small cvs; layers a hundred means out lose about log10(x/μ) digits.
* [The tail in the bootstraps, refitted or drawn, against Mack's tail risk](bootstrap-tail-parameter-error.md) - A constant tail drawn with Mack's tail standard error and given Mack's tail sigma reproduces R's MackChainLadder(tail = ...) standard errors; a tail refitted on each simulation's pseudo factors has R's process error but its own parameter error (RAA's oldest origin 1.56 times first order), of the order of R's extrapolated tail.se, not equal to it.
* [The one-year view with a bootstrap tail](one-year-bootstrap-tail.md) - Valuing the tail step one development period after the oldest age closes an annual origin at the oldest age in the coming year; with a constant tail Mack's bootstrap gives the open origins 1.05 times R's CDR(1)S.E., the oldest R's Mack.S.E with the tail, within 2.2 Monte Carlo SE on RAA, GenIns and ABC; R's CDR rejects tails, so the targets follow from the chosen semantics.
* [The Bayesian ODP chain ladder against Stan, the chain ladder and the ODP bootstrap](bayes-odp-vs-odp-bootstrap.md) - BayesOdp matches the same model in Stan on GenIns and RAA at 30 of 30 seeds, within tolerances of 4 times its own Monte Carlo error (on RAA's young origins about twice GenIns'); on GenIns its mean reserve is 0.7% above the chain ladder and 0.3% below the ODP bootstrap, its sd 2.5% below the bootstrap's.
* [The Bayesian Bornhuetter–Ferguson against Stan, EVW (2012) and its limits](bayes-bf-parity.md) - BayesBf matches the same model in Stan at 200 of 200 seeds within 4.7 times its Monte Carlo error, reproduces EVW's printed totals, and moves from BayesOdp's reserve to EVW's strong-prior limit; a tight prior needed the mode finder fixed, and a flat prior beside a very vague pattern prior diverges.
* [Meyers' CRC and CSR against his Stan code and his printed results](meyers-crc-csr-parity.md) - Meyers matches Meyers' transcribed Stan code on group 353 at 30 of 30 seeds within 4 times its Monte Carlo error, and his printed totals within 0.3%; it needs his 1e-5 floor on the variance steps and a pilot-run scale, since the joint mode lies deep in the late sigmas' funnel.
* [Meyers' CAY against his Stan code and his printed results](meyers-cay-parity.md) - MeyersModel::Cay matches the transcribed CAY.R on group 353 incurred at 200 of 200 seeds within 4.1 times its Monte Carlo error and his printed results; with sigma[10] near 0.013 the prediction's recursion is invisible to the parity, so a unit test checks it.
* [The Bayesian ODP over segments and in the diagonal backtest](bayes-odp-segments-backtest.md) - A derived seed per segment makes twin lines independent (Spearman sd 0.011 over 40 seeds); rank correlation reaches its targets within 0.009; in the backtest BayesOdp agrees with the ODP GLM on the CLRD lines and forecasts about 1% higher on GenIns; Newton needed a scale-aware rounding floor on lines summed over companies.
* [Meyers' models over segments and in the diagonal backtest](meyers-segments-backtest.md) - A derived seed per segment makes twin lines independent (Spearman sd 0.0083 over 60 seeds); rank correlation moves ultimates with reserves and reaches its targets within 0.0081; in the backtest CRC, CSR and CAY over-forecast group 353's held-out diagonals (A/E 0.30 to 0.90) and cover every held-out cell, beating the ODP models' log density on wkcomp and trailing it on comauto and group 353.
