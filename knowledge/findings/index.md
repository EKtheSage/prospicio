# Findings

* [Normal draws bias the log-link mean](odp-normal-draws-bias.md) - Drawing beta from N(beta_hat, Sigma) and exponentiating raises each mean by exp(x'Sigma x / 2); MeanPreserving draws remove it exactly.
* [Negative increments in the ODP](odp-negative-increments.md) - A quasi-likelihood ODP needs only V(mu) = mu and mu > 0; negative responses are fitted with a quasi-deviance that is not a distance.
* [ODP log density](odp-continuous-density.md) - The ODP's lattice probability is -inf off the lattice; its continuation through Gamma, normalized by C(lambda), is a proper density in y.
* [Stacking pooling funnel](stacking-pooling-funnel.md) - With one slope per pooled group, the group scale and the slope trade off; NUTS diverges. Three or more covariates mix well.
* [R callbacks must stay on R's main thread](r-callbacks-main-thread.md) - An R function held inside a Rust object may be called only from R's thread; parallel code that meets one runs in order on the calling thread.
* [Reinstatements pro rata as to time](reinstatement-pro-rata-time.md) - Each event's used limit is charged at the share of the year left after it; sorted uniform (or seasonal) times in drawn order date i.i.d. losses exactly; one exhausting loss a year costs half the amount-only premium.
* [Bondy least squares](bondy-least-squares-stop.md) - TailBondy fits b with scipy least_squares at default tolerances, which stop on a 1e-8 relative cost change; b is off by up to 4e-6, the tail's results by up to 3e-5.
* [The simulated one-year view against Merz-Wuthrich, ODP and Mack's process](one-year-bootstrap-vs-merz-wuthrich.md) - Re-reserving on the ODP bootstrap gives 0.50 to 5.96 times Merz-Wuthrich's one-year SD per origin; under Mack's process (MackBootstrap) the same re-reserving reconciles its standard deviation with Merz-Wuthrich within Monte Carlo error on RAA, GenIns and ABC and matches EVW (2019) Table 4, so the gap is the ODP's process model; its mean CDR is biased unless the residuals are centred, the default. A quarterly split of an annual triangle gets about half its one-year SD.
* [Mack's bootstrap, lifetime view, against Mack's standard errors and EVW Table 4](mack-bootstrap-lifetime-vs-mack.md) - MackBootstrap::fit reproduces Mack's standard error on RAA, GenIns and ABC only with centred residuals, its parameter error scaled by the residuals' variance 1 - m^2; uncentred, the mean reserve is biased (RAA +17%); EVW's Table 4 expected reserves agree with centred residuals, the default.
* [Stream scheme and samplers, two records in a result's provenance](provenance-samplers.md) - Seeded results record the sampler versions (gamma = marsaglia-tsang/2026-10) apart from the stream scheme; join's seed check compares seed and scheme, replay also samplers; results saved before 2026-10-08 carry no samplers.
* [The Gamma sampler, Marsaglia-Tsang instead of inverse transform](gamma-sampler.md) - Gamma::sample draws by Marsaglia and Tsang (2000) on the same streams: 0.13 to 0.22 microseconds a draw at any shape against up to 375 by bisecting the cdf; every Gamma draw changed and is no longer monotone in one uniform.
* [The inverse Gaussian without e^(2λ/μ)](inverse-gaussian-mills-ratio.md) - With the Mills ratio R, F = Φ(a) + φ(a) R(b) and S = φ(a)(R(a) - R(b)), exactly, so nothing overflows at small cvs; layers a hundred means out lose about log10(x/μ) digits.
