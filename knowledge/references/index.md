# Reference implementations

* [statsmodels](statsmodels.md) - Python statsmodels, the reference for prospicio-glm's GLMs, robust standard errors and Tweedie profiles; its conventions and quirks.
* [R loo](r-loo.md) - The R package loo 2.6.0, the reference for ELPD by PSIS-LOO and WAIC, and 2.10.1 for the LOO-PIT by E_loo; its stacking_weights stops early.
* [BayesBlend](bayesblend.md) - Ledger Investing's BayesBlend 0.0.8 (MIT), whose Stan models prospicio_bayes::stacking follows, including partial pooling and adaptive priors.
* [R Pareto](r-pareto.md) - The R package Pareto 2.4.5 (GPL), used only for reference values; where its tower matching departs from Riegel (2018).
* [nuts-rs](nuts-rs.md) - nutpie's Rust core, nuts-rs 0.19 (MIT), the sampler behind prospicio_bayes::nuts, BayesGlm and stacking; how prospicio-bayes drives it.
* [R ChainLadder CDR](r-chainladder-cdr.md) - R ChainLadder 0.2.21's CDR.MackChainLadder, the reference for Merz-Wuthrich's claims development result; its conventions, and where it and the 2008 paper differ.
* [Tails in R ChainLadder and chainladder-python](chainladder-tails.md) - R's tail = TRUE rule and tail_SE, chainladder-python's TailConstant, TailCurve and TailBondy; the quirks prospicio_reserving::Tail reproduces or avoids.
* [LightGBM and XGBoost](lightgbm-xgboost.md) - The engines behind prospicio.boosting; how offsets, starting scores and precision behave, and which wheels to install.
* [MBBEFD exposure curves](mbbefd.md) - The MBBEFD class behind prospicio_pricing::exposure; its four closed-form cases, the Swiss Re c curves, and numerical points found while building it.
* [chainladder-python expected-loss estimators](chainladder-python-expected-loss.md) - chainladder-python 0.10.1's ExpectedLoss, BornhuetterFerguson, Benktander and CapeCod, the reference for prospicio_reserving's expected-loss family; how its arithmetic and Cape Cod trend work.
* [Selected development patterns in chainladder-python and R ChainLadder](chainladder-python-development-constant.md) - chainladder-python 0.10.1's DevelopmentConstant, the parity reference for prospicio_reserving's SelectedPattern: no blend, factors of 1 where a partial pattern gives none, a cdf pattern's level kept as a tail unless a TailConstant replaces it, rejected by MackChainladder; R's MackChainLadder takes no factors.
* [Averages over the latest periods and excluding high and low in chainladder-python and R ChainLadder](chainladder-python-development-exclusions.md) - chainladder-python 0.10.1's Development(n_periods, drop_high, drop_low) and R's MackChainLadder(weights = W), the parity references for Development's n_periods and drops: calendar diagonals, ties by the starting value, drops skipped where they would leave none, w_ counting one cell too many, no geometric average.
* [chainladder-python's BootstrapODPSample under n_periods and the drops](chainladder-python-bootstrap-odp.md) - chainladder-python 0.10.1's BootstrapODPSample(n_periods, drop_high, drop_low), the parity reference for the ODP bootstrap under exclusions; its first-age weight quirk, its unreduced scale denominator, its dropped zeros and centred pool, hat_adj's default, and its unrefitted resamples.
* [Property exposure curves beyond MBBEFD](property-exposure-curves.md) - First-loss scales, PSOLD, Lloyd's and reinsurer curves, and how prospicio_pricing::exposure covers them with MBBEFD, tabulated and severity curves.
* [R ChainLadder ClarkLDF and ClarkCapeCod](r-chainladder-clark.md) - R ChainLadder 0.2.21's Clark growth-curve methods, the reference for prospicio_reserving's ClarkLdf and ClarkCapeCod; their age, scale and reporting definitions, a loose optimizer stop, a silent cap of 10 on the Cape Cod ELR, and a wrong Weibull second derivative.
* [chainladder-python ClarkLDF](chainladder-python-clark.md) - chainladder-python 0.10.1's ClarkLDF, the secondary reference for prospicio_reserving's Clark methods; what it shares with R ChainLadder and where its outputs are not comparable.
* [Error and uncertainty terminology](error-terminology.md) - What standard error, process, parameter, model, systemic and systematic error, epistemic and aleatoric uncertainty, bias and variance mean, where they overlap, and which prospicio-reserving outputs measure which.
* [Mildenhall's aggregate package, Pricing Insurance Risk and CAS Monograph 15](aggregate-mildenhall.md) - The Python aggregate package (1.0.1) as the reference for distortions, calibration, natural allocation and contract terms; where the Pricing Insurance Risk and Monograph 15 examples live, and the API and tolerance quirks met reproducing them.
* [Mildenhall and Major, Pricing Insurance Risk, Part III (price allocation), reproduced in prospicio](pir-price-allocation.md) - Chapters 12 to 15 and 19 recomputed by two guides: the book's linear allocation lumps an outcome at the asset level with the defaults (prospicio matches at a - 1e-9), two covariance allocations, Example 302's net view, and Table 15.36's CCoC row, which is below the CCoC floor and so not a natural allocation.
* [Major and Mildenhall, CAS Monograph 15, chapters 3 to 6 reproduced in prospicio](major-mildenhall-monograph-15-chapters-3-6.md) - The InsCo tables of the theory and applications chapters recomputed by two guides; every table reproduces, the last-digit differences are aggregate's calibration tolerance and rounded intermediates, and algorithm 3.4.2 reads VaR at a jump as the upper quantile.
* [Major and Mildenhall, CAS Monograph 15, chapters 1, 2, 7 and 8 reproduced in prospicio](major-mildenhall-monograph-15-chapters-1-2-7-8.md) - Iman-Conover Examples 1 and 2, block Iman-Conover, capital by layer and Bodoff on InsCo; exact tables match, sampled ones agree within sampling error, the output of iman_conover has a sorted first column, and three conventions for the bottom layer give three CCoC capital splits.
* [aggregate 1.0.1's reinsurance pricing examples, what its numbers mean and where prospicio matches them](aggregate-reinsurance-pricing.md) - The reference file behind examples/reinsurance_pricing.ipynb, six contracts built in aggregate 1.0.1 (tower, program and share, aggregate limit with reinstatements, stop loss and deductible, variable features, distortion pricing), how aggregate defines each number, and the quirks met matching them with prospicio (skewness deficit, share re-bucketing, tail lumping, calibration error).
* [Mildenhall and Major, Pricing Insurance Risk, Parts I and II, reproduced in prospicio](pricing-insurance-risk-parts-1-2.md) - Chapters 3 to 11's printed numbers recomputed by the guides pir-risk-measures.md and pir-portfolio-pricing.md: the grids that reproduce them, and the cells that do not (a wrong lognormal mu, TVaR of stop-lossed units, a factor of 10 in Table 9.15, Esscher and exponential on a lognormal).
* [Werner and Modlin's Basic Ratemaking](werner-modlin-ratemaking.md) - The CAS study note behind prospicio_pricing::ratemaking; how its exhibits round, its half-month dates, and the book errors met.
* [Werner and Modlin's Basic Ratemaking, reproduced in the ratemaking guides](werner-modlin-guides.md) - What the two guides found beyond the parity suite: Table 6.12 averages rounded link ratios (1.30 for 1.2932), Appendix A's high-low development average as a selected pattern, Table 12.1's z of 2.575, the Buhlmann example via credibility_premiums, and chapter 11's size-of-loss arithmetic.
* [Chalk, Deacon, Guillen and Martinelli, CAS Monograph 16, reproduced in the guides](chalk-monograph-16.md) - What the two Monograph 16 guides found: the case-study data is not offline, chapter 8's offset example reproduces from its printed cell counts, three printed slips, and ElasticNet's slowness on hinge bases.
* [Goldburd, Khare, Tevet and Guller's GLMs for Insurance Rating, reproduced in the GLM ratemaking guides](goldburd-glm-monograph-5.md) - CAS Monograph 5: its printed arithmetic matched (F = 2.412 at 95.2%, Table 12 from Table 11 within rounding, Table 5's 0.88), every coefficient table on data not in the PDF, three wrong figure references, footnote 17's AUROC-Gini identity checked with gini.
* [R actuar's credibility models](actuar-credibility.md) - actuar 3.3.7's cm(), the reference for prospicio_pricing::credibility and prospicio_bayes::credibility; its one-level estimators, its hierarchy (~cohort + cohort:state, variances named by the level above), credibility-weighted collectives and Hachemeister's data.
* [The Bayesian ODP chain ladder in Stan](stan-bayes-odp-reference.md) - validation/scripts/stan/bayes_odp.stan in CmdStan 2.39.0, the reference for BayesOdp; plug-ins passed as data, RAA needs adapt_delta 0.99 to sample without divergences.
* [England, Verrall and Wüthrich (2012), the Bayesian ODP and the Bornhuetter–Ferguson](evw-2012-bayesian-bf.md) - the model BayesBf builds; its strong-prior limit is Mack's raw-pattern BF, not the classical BF, and its printed totals on the Wüthrich–Merz triangle reproduce within their Monte Carlo error.
* [Meyers' CRC, CSR and CAY code and its Stan transcription](meyers-monograph-8.md) - CAS Monograph 8's Stan programs, unlicensed and no longer compiling on CmdStan 2.39, transcribed for the Meyers reference; prior sd 3.162, a 1e-5 floor on each variance step, reversed sigma labels, pmax and ceiling, elpd on the log scale; CAY's recursion runs on cells sorted by age, then origin.
* [Meyers, Stochastic Loss Reserving Using Bayesian MCMC Models (CAS Monograph 8), reproduced in prospicio](meyers-monograph-8-reproduced.md) - Group 353's printed Mack, ODP, CRC, CSR and CAY results recomputed by the guide reserving-meyers.md, all within Monte Carlo error over 20 seeds; the outcome percentiles, Table 5.1's scrambled text layer, and why a CI-checked guide prints tolerances rather than NUTS digits.
* [Gesmann and Morris (2020), hierarchical compartmental reserving models](gesmann-morris-2020.md) - The CAS Research Paper behind Compartmental; its brms code, reconstructed as the Stan program brms generates, reproduces the printed model 1 posterior to rounding and model 2's within 0.08 printed sd on company 337; text and code disagree on the origin hierarchy and on model 2's sigma prior, and the code wins for parity.
* [Gesmann and Morris (2020), compartmental models 1 and 2, reproduced in the guide](gesmann-morris-2020-reproduced.md) - The guide reserving-compartmental.md fits models 1 and 2 on company 337: every printed m1fit row agrees (checked in CI), m2fit within 0.08 printed sd on every mean (recorded); the outcome sits at the 92nd (model 1) and 99th (model 2) percentile, the young years' loss ratios shrunk toward the population's.
* [Burn 0.21's activations in f64](burn.md) - The framework behind prospicio-nn; its default sigmoid and hard_sigmoid compute in f32 on an f64 backend, gelu's backward pass is the tanh approximation's, and prospicio-nn uses neither.
* [onnx-ir 0.21, Burn's ONNX parser, at run time](onnx-ir.md) - The parser behind burn-import loads ONNX at run time, builds without protoc, fuses MatMul and Add into Linear and holds Rc values; prospicio_nn::onnx copies it into an owned plan.
* [Clark's Basics of Reinsurance Pricing](clark-reinsurance-pricing.md) - The CAS study note behind prospicio_pricing::experience; its experience rating example's day count (actual/365.25), rounding, development by year and total as a ratio of sums.
* [pyvinecopulib 1.0.1, the reference for prospicio's vine copulas](pyvinecopulib.md) - Rotations and h-functions match prospicio's; its C-vine order puts the root last and as each edge's second argument, and its inverse Rosenblatt runs through a D-vine's order backwards.
* [mgcv 1.9.1's REML score, the reference for prospicio's REML smoothing](mgcv-reml.md) - gcv.ubre is the Laplace-approximate restricted likelihood with the observed information and reml.scale (not sig2) as the dispersion; mgcv stops at about 3e-7 relative, short of the minimum where the score is flat.
* [R ismev's threshold diagnostics](r-ismev.md) - ismev 1.43's gpd.fit, gpd.fitrange and mrl.plot, the reference for prospicio_prob::evt::threshold_diagnostics; Nelder-Mead puts its estimates within about 1e-3 of the exact maximum, its parameter order is (scale, shape), and mrl.plot's bands use the sample standard deviation.
* [mgcv 1.9.1's cyclic P-spline, bs = "cp"](mgcv-cyclic.md) - How mgcv builds s(x, bs = "cp"), the reference for prospicio_glm's cyclic smooths: k + 1 unwidened knots, functions wrapped by cSplineDes, a cyclic difference penalty, new data wrapped by cwrap, and smoothCon rescaling the penalty unless scale.penalty = FALSE.
* [scam 1.2-22's monotone P-splines, bs = "mpi" and "mpd"](scam-monotone.md) - How scam builds and fits shape-constrained smooths, the reference for prospicio_glm's monotone smooths: a cumulative-sum basis centered by column means, exp increments, a scaled penalty, a Newton-Hessian edf, and a BFGS that stops short of the GCV minimum.
* [aggregate 1.2.0 documentation against prospicio, a coverage map](aggregate-docs-coverage.md) - What the aggregate 1.2.0 documentation offers, where prospicio matches it and the gaps (shared mixing, grid audit, severity shift and scale, DecL front end).
* [Bahnemann, Distributions for Actuaries (CAS Monograph 2), reproduced in prospicio](bahnemann-distributions-for-actuaries.md) - The monograph's parameterisations against prospicio's (its Pareto is a Lomax, its gamma has a scale, its negative binomial is in mean and contagion) and the printed numbers that do not reproduce: rounding carried into differences, a misprinted mu in Ex 6.6, solver results, and shifted-gamma aggregate-limit credits several times the exact ones.
* [Friedland's Estimating Unpaid Claims Using Basic Techniques](friedland-estimating-unpaid-claims.md) - The CAS text on basic reserving reproduced in docs/guides/reserving-friedland.md; its exhibits use three-decimal rounded CDFs, claims held with decimals behind whole-thousand printing, CDFs capped at 1 for BF and Cape Cod on XYZ, and an unrounded Cape Cod ratio.
* [Taylor and McGuire, Stochastic Loss Reserving Using GLMs (CAS Monograph 3), reproduced in prospicio](taylor-mcguire-monograph-3.md) - The chain ladder as an ODP GLM on the NJ Manufacturers workers compensation triangle: every printed estimate, standard error and delta-method RMSEP reproduces; the parametric bootstrap's higher means are lognormal bias, and Table 7-7's AIC and BIC sit about one unit off.
