# Datasets

* [freMTPL2freq](fremtpl2.md) - 678,013 French motor TPL policies with claim counts and exposure; the GLM parity portfolio, fetched from OpenML, not committed.
* [RAA triangle](raa.md) - Mack's (1993) 10x10 cumulative paid triangle; has one negative increment, which shapes how ODP models must handle it.
