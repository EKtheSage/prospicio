# Datasets

* [CAS loss reserve database, six lines](clrd-lines.md) - Paid triangles and net earned premium of comauto, medmal, othliab, ppauto, prodliab and wkcomp summed over companies (chainladder-python 0.10.1), 1988-1997; same shape, so they can be synchronized.
* [freMTPL2freq and freMTPL2sev](fremtpl2.md) - 678,013 French motor TPL policies with claim counts and exposure, and their 26,639 claim amounts; the GLM parity portfolio, fetched from OpenML, not committed.
* [RAA triangle](raa.md) - Mack's (1993) 10x10 cumulative paid triangle; has one negative increment, which shapes how ODP models must handle it.
* [Meyers' illustrative insurer, group 353 Commercial Auto](clrd-comauto-353.md) - The full 10x10 square (paid, case incurred, net earned premium) of CAS group 353 from the CAS Schedule P data model release 20260613_041006; matches Meyers' Tables 2.1-2.3 but for one misprint, outcomes paid 40,000 and incurred 40,061.
* [A quarterly-development triangle simulated from compartmental model 1](compartmental-sim-quarterly.md) - Ten annual accident years 2011-2020 with quarterly development, 220 outstanding and 220 paid cells, simulated from Gesmann and Morris' model 1 with known parameters; the repository's only unequal-grain triangle, for the compartmental model's unequal-grain parity and recovery tests.
* [Gesmann and Morris' company 337, workers' compensation](clrd-wkcomp-337.md) - The full 10x10 square (paid, incurred with bulk and IBNR, case incurred, direct and net premium) of NAIC company 337 from the CAS Schedule P data model release 20260613_041006; the papers' outstanding is incurred less paid and their premium direct, as the 1988 cell at age 1 shows.
* [Synthetic reinsurance submission, commercial property per risk](synthetic-submission.md) - A seeded, generated submission for a 2026 per-risk excess of loss program (premium and rate changes, 168 listed large claims, a loss triangle, a risk profile, treaty terms, an answer key outside the submission's folder); examples/submission_evaluation.ipynb recovers alpha 1.720 against a true 1.6 (1.24 when the per-claim reporting thresholds are ignored) and misses the counts' dispersion (0.80 against 1.97).
* [Meyers' 200-triangle Schedule P panel](clrd-panel.md) - The 50 triangles per line (CA, PA, WC, OL) Meyers' screen selects, rebuilt at run time from the cas-schedule-p package and not stored; Table A.2 prints only 175 of the 200 codes, so Mack reproducing Meyers' D is the check.
* [Synthetic property-catastrophe book, Larkspur Re](synthetic-cat-book.md) - A seeded catastrophe book for the cat-* guides (three cedents, four perils, an ELT and 2,000 simulated years of dated events, five contracts); at p = 0.99 the k-th largest year (177.979) differs from the inverted-cdf VaR (176.824), and a 72-hour clause turns Alder's 5,391 losses into 4,238 occurrences, 87 of them joining catalog events.
