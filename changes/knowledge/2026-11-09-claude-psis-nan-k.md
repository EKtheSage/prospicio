* **Update**: [Compartmental on sub-annual data](/findings/compartmental-subannual.md), from #782 (branch claude/psis-nan-k): the NaN Pareto k of the quarterly one-year view was `exp` underflow, not ties; `psis` now returns an infinite k for such a tail. In the default view of the low-noise quarterly case 18 of 200 simulations have that infinite k, as many as had a NaN one; under `MomentMatchThenRefit` they now reach moment matching (none rescued) before their refit.
