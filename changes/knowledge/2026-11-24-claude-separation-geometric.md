* **Update**: [Taylor (1977), the separation method, and its worked example](/references/taylor-1977-separation.md), from #979 (branch claude/separation-geometric): the geometric separation method (least squares on `ln s_ij` with development and calendar factors, proportions normalised to sum to 1) equals statsmodels' OLS on the logs to 1e-9 on GenIns and ABC, with and without a premium normaliser; the lognormal retransformation multiplies every calendar index by `exp(sigma^2 / 2)` with `sigma^2 = RSS / df`, and on a full n by n triangle `df = (n - 1)(n - 2) / 2`, so a 2 by 2 triangle has none and its lognormal mean is undefined.
