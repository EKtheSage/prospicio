* **Update**: [The ESG's correlated Gaussian factors](/findings/esg-correlated-factors.md), from #983 (branch claude/project-thread-n5mttf-esg-inflation): two Ornstein-Uhlenbeck factors and their integrals over a step are one exact Gaussian draw with covariances `ρ ∫ f g` of their kernels; a short step makes the noises nearly collinear, so the factor is a semidefinite Cholesky; and a year-end shock regressed on the year's Brownian increments has the given correlation with each.
