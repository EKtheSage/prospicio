* **Correction**: [CHF inversion](/findings/chf-inversion-aggregate.md): a CGMY law with Y < 0 is compound Poisson with an atom exp(-C Gamma(-Y) (M^Y + G^Y)) at mu. `aggregate` 2.1.0 does not take it out, so its masses ring near mu (3e-3 in the bucket at mu for C=2, G=3, M=4, Y=-0.5, log2=12); prospicio-prob now does, and the parity row for that law skips the truncation error and the masses near mu (#944).
