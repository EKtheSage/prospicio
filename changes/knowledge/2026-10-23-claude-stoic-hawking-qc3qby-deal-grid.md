* **Note**: a deal's scales on the grid (branch claude/stoic-hawking-qc3qby, `[deal-grid]`): the deal's key is the sum of its sections' keys over their fixed premiums, so a compound grid gives it only when every section's cession is a function of each loss (no annual terms); with annual terms the sections' annual totals are dependent and no single compound gives their sum. Two 50% placements of one cover in one deal equal one 100% placement with the deal's scale as its own, on the compound grid, on the annual grid and in the cedant's ledger, to 1e-12.
