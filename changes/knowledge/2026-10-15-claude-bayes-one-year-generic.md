* **Creation**: [The Bayesian Bornhuetter-Ferguson's one-year view between the chain ladder and the strong-prior limit](/findings/bayes-bf-one-year.md), from building `BayesBf::one_year` and the generic one-year engine (branch claude/bayes-one-year-generic, `docs/design/reserving-bayes.md` decision 17): a flat prior matches `BayesOdp`'s view, a tight one the closed form of the strong-prior limit, and the total's sd falls to 70% at cv 0.2 and 50% at cv 0.05 on GenIns.
