* **Creation**: [A factor to ultimate at one age is the tail from that age](/findings/one-value-cumulative-pattern.md), from issue #632 (branch claude/one-value-pattern, `reserving-v02.md` decision 20): a cumulative pattern with one value is the tail at the oldest age with no tail given, so a book observed at one age projects by it alone (one origin at 12 months, 200 paid, `{12: 3.0}`: 600), and on RAA `{120: 1.05}` is the constant tail of 1.05 to 1e-15; a longer pattern that ends at a one-age triangle's only age is its level there (800 paid at 48 months with the pattern from 12 to 48 months: 896); at another age, with a tail (on a latest diagonal also `PastPatternEnd::Tail` or `NoDevelopment`, whatever the origins' ages) or at a weight below 1 it is an error.
