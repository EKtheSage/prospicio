* **Update**: [The Solvency II counterparty default module for type 1 exposures](/references/solvency2-counterparty-default.md), from #980 PR 6 (branch claude/reinsurer-default-980-term): each constant now cites its article paragraph, and every constant of `solvency2.rs` and `aggregate_reinsurer_default.py` was rechecked on 2026-11-24 against the EU consolidated text of Delegated Regulation (EU) 2015/35 of 2024-11-14 (the text in force; EUR-Lex's pages did not load, so its XHTML came from the Publications Office cellar), all matching. The consolidation of 2027-01-30 adds Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/269: from that date the 90 % case of Art 192(2) uses the factor F''' (100 % or 90 %) instead of F' (100 % or 18 %), the fix EIOPA Q&A 3119 expected, and Art 201(2)(a) excludes a pair of zero probabilities, as Q&A 2530 already did. The code takes the factor as an argument, so it holds under both texts; the docs now say which factor applies when. Each `type1` entry must be one single name exposure (Art 199(1), 201(3)). A unit test pins Art 200's "lower than or equal to" at sigma exactly on 7 % and 20 % of the total loss given default. Default over a term taught nothing new beyond the design note: with a constant probability `p` and independent years a reinsurer is in default by year `t` with probability `1 - (1 - p)^t`, which `validation/tests/aggregate_reinsurer_default_term.rs` confirms with and without the factor.
