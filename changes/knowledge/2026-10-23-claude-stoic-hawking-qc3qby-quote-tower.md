* **Note**: the whole-tower quote (branch claude/stoic-hawking-qc3qby): a tower's total cession is one compound total on the FFT grid only when no layer has annual terms or the tower is a single stage with a total; paid reinstatements on two layers already make it a sum of dependent annual totals, so `TowerGrids::total_ceded` is `None` there and the total is quoted on simulated events. On the R example tower (1 xs 1 and 2 xs 2, Poisson 2, TVaR 99% assets, 10% brokerage) writing the layers together saves 1.8% of their technical deposits at a 10% cost of capital and 2.0% at 12%.
