* **Note**: [Werner and Modlin's Basic Ratemaking](/references/werner-modlin-ratemaking.md), from the R twins of the ratemaking guides (branch guides/werner-modlin-ratemaking-r): the R package covers every step the Python guides use (`rate_level(level = ...)` for printed indices, `chain_ladder(ldf = ..., ldf_kind = "ldf")`, `credibility_premiums()` with a tight prior for the book's Buhlmann example) and prints the same numbers; R's `round()` is half-even on the binary value, so the guides round half up with their own `half_up()` after clearing noise at 10 places.
