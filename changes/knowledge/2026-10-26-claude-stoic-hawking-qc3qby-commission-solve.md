* **Note**: a deposit solved under loss-sensitive commissions (branch claude/stoic-hawking-qc3qby-commission-solve, `pareto.md`, "A deposit solved under loss-sensitive commissions"): on a quota share ceding 0 to 100 in steps of 20 (expected loss 50, TVaR 99% assets, 10% cost of capital, risk premium 54.55, 2% expenses) with a sliding scale from 35% at a 50% loss ratio to 20% at 70%, the solved deposit is 76.870 with an expected commission of 20.787 (27.0% of it), between the flat quotes at the scale's two ends (86.580 at 35%, 69.930 at 20%); a 20% profit commission after a 10% allowance gives 57.916 with 2.212 of commission. The kept amount need not rise with the deposit inside a band whose intercept exceeds what the loads leave, so a steep scale can meet the risk premium at several deposits.
