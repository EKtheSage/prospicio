* **Creation**: [An annual accident-year pattern read at monthly ages as a Weibull corrected to its own yearly averages converts to an underwriting year within 0.2% where decision 18's reading is 31% off](/findings/ay-pattern-monthly-reading.md), from the design study for issue #646 (branch claude/ay-monthly-reading, `reserving-v02.md` decision 25, proposed): an accident year's share is the average of its months, so a reading between annual ages is a choice of the monthly pattern; the Weibull fitted through that average and moved to the nearest monotone monthly pattern that reproduces the given values exactly gives ratios 0.9989 to 1.0000 at 12 to 60 months on the simulated book (1.3116 and 0.9155 today), is exact on Weibull truths and 1.5% off at 12 months on a slow loglogistic; on CLRD and Friedland patterns the exact monotone readings agree within 4% to 12% at 12 months and today's reading is 20% to 100% above them.
