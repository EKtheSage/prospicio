* **Creation**: [An annual accident-year pattern read at monthly ages as a Weibull corrected to its own yearly averages converts to an underwriting year within 0.2% where decision 18's reading is 31% off](/findings/ay-pattern-monthly-reading.md), from the design study and build for issue #646 (branch claude/ay-monthly-reading, `reserving-v02.md` decision 25, accepted by the user on 2026-10-27 as the default of `to_underwriting_basis`): an accident year's share is the average of its months, so a reading between annual ages is a choice of the monthly pattern; the Weibull fitted through that average and moved to the nearest monotone monthly pattern that reproduces the given values exactly gives ratios 0.9989 to 1.0000 at 12 to 60 months on the simulated book (1.3116 and 0.9155 under decision 18's reading, now `AnnualReading::LnLinear`), verified in CI by `validation/tests/reserving_uy_pattern.rs`, where the Rust reading matches the scipy prototype within 1.1e-9 on the book and CLRD private passenger auto paid; it is exact on Weibull truths and 1.5% off at 12 months on a slow loglogistic; on CLRD and Friedland patterns the exact monotone readings agree within 2% to 12% at 12 months and decision 18's reading is 19% to 86% above them. As built the study's cap of the monthly pattern at the level is dropped (it flattened the last window and left accident years at quarterly ages still developing at their end with no solution), and the projection reads 6,000 seeded monotone patterns at ages finer than their accident period without a failure.
