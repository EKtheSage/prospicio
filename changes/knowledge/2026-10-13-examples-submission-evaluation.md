* **Creation**: [Synthetic reinsurance submission, commercial property per risk](/datasets/synthetic-submission.md), from writing `examples/submission_evaluation.ipynb` (branch examples/submission-evaluation): a seeded generator writes 168 listed large claims, a triangle, a premium and rate history, a risk profile, treaty terms and an answer key (kept outside the submission's folder); the notebook recovers alpha 1.720 against a true 1.6 (1.24 if the per-claim reporting thresholds are ignored), the cost trend and exposure growth to 0.1%, and layer losses within 1% to 17% of the truth, where experience gets credibility 0.16, 0.07 and 0.03 and the Swiss Re curve parameter and the counts' dispersion (estimated 0.80, true 1.97) decide the price. `test_submission_evaluation` runs the notebook on every PR and asserts looser forms of these; `verified` follows the CI run that merges it.
* **Update**: [Evaluating a reinsurance submission with prospicio, what the API covers](/findings/program-evaluation-api-map.md), from running every step end to end (branch examples/submission-evaluation): no library object for a severity cut at a mix of policy limits, `Pareto.fit` returns no standard error, `fit_references` prints `NaN` for the references it refuses, `RiskProfile` refuses a band with `lower=0`, `year_fraction` puts 31 December in the next year, `CapeCod`'s trended prior is at the valuation date's prices; a count triangle through `ChainLadder` and a Poisson `Glm` with an offset work for large-loss frequency; development factors measured on every listed claim are biased upward by the listing's selection on the latest incurred; at a 99% TVaR stand-alone standard the top layer costs 2.3 times its price under one distortion. `test_submission_evaluation` runs the notebook on every PR; the numbers are from a local run.
