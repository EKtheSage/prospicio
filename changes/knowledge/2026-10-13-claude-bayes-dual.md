* **Update**: [Automatic differentiation for small Bayesian reserving models, benchmarked](/findings/autodiff-benchmark.md), from building the `Scalar` trait and the in-house dual type (branch claude/bayes-dual, `docs/design/reserving-compartmental.md` PR A): with chunked passes at a runtime parameter count, 32 tangents a pass is fastest or within noise at 27, 29 and 90 parameters, a gradient of Meyers' CRC costs 2.3 to 2.5 times the hand-written one (within the 10 times accepted), and the generic densities of `BayesOdp`, `BayesBf` and `Meyers` match the hand-written values and gradients to 1e-12 relative at 20 points each; a dual `tanh` needs `1 / cosh²`, and the closed form's kernel needs its series branch up to `|x| < 1e-2` for an accurate dual derivative. The oracle tests are not yet run by CI; the benchmark is an ignored test, not run by CI.
