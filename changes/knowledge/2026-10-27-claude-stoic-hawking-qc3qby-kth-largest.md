* **Update**: [Sample quantile and TVaR conventions](/findings/quantile-and-tvar-conventions.md), the k-th largest year and the whole-years TVaR (#582, branch claude/stoic-hawking-qc3qby-kth-largest): the k-th largest is the upper quantile, one order statistic above the inverted cdf; the whole-years TVaR equals the exact one when (1 - p) N is whole; `floor((1 - p) N)` in floating point loses a year at p = 0.9, N = 10, so k is taken as N - rank(N, p).
