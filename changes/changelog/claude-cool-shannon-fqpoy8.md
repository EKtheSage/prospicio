### Pricing

- A bound deal valued from its actual accounts (`time-value.md` decision 34): `CashflowTerms::valuation` takes an `ActualState` at a month (premium collected, losses paid, the trust's and operating account's balances, capital funded, equity released, optionally the investor's flows to date and the funding reference) and `Valuation::run` simulates the rest of the term from it, each simulation's ultimate conditioned on the paid to date by `condition_on_paid` (`Conditioning::BornhuetterFerguson`, the default, keeps every simulation at the actual paid plus its own unpaid; `Truncation` keeps those whose ultimate reaches the paid). `Forward` gives the flows with the return from the valuation month (the net asset value as the investment) and since inception (the actual flows first): rows, NPV, IRR and MOIC of each. `deal_ave` gives the actual against expected paid by month, the actual's percentile among the priced simulations and quantiles, with a paid pattern by simulation when given. A valuation at month 0 is `run`. `ActualState::from_trace` takes one simulated path's state, and `Trace` reports the funding reference and the opening balance. Python `CashflowTerms.valuation`, `CashflowTerms.actual_state`, `Valuation`, `Forward`, `condition_on_paid`, `deal_ave`; R `cashflow_valuation()`, `cashflow_actual_state()`, `cashflow_forward()`, `cashflow_valuation_trace()`, `condition_on_paid()`, `deal_ave()` (#603).
- **Breaking:** `Trace` has two new public fields, `funding_reference` and `opening_balance`, so a `Trace` struct literal must add them; `cashflow_trace()`'s `accounts` in R and `CashflowTerms.trace`'s dict in Python have a `funding_reference` column (#603).
