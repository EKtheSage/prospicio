### Excel

- `PRO.YLT.STATS`, `PRO.YLT.OEP`, `PRO.ELT.STATS` and `PRO.ELT.OEP`: a year loss table's annual totals (average annual loss, SD, VaR and TVaR under a stated quantile rule) and its occurrence and aggregate exceedance curves, by return period or at amounts, with an optional hours clause; an event loss table's expected annual loss and occurrence exceedance curve with secondary uncertainty (#578).
