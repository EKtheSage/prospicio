### Excel

- `PRO.CAP.CHF`: a law's distribution on an FFT grid from its
  characteristic function, as `aggregate`'s `AggregateCHF`: the normal,
  gamma, inverse Gaussian, Tweedie at any power, alpha-stable (S1 or S0,
  its power tail tilted, a two-sided one split into halves), normal
  inverse Gaussian, variance gamma and CGMY tempered stable laws, with
  the window's mean, SD, VaR and TVaR and the inversion's errors, or each
  grid point's mass (#779, #780).
- `PRO.CAP.STABLE` and `PRO.CAP.STABLE.PARETO`: an alpha-stable law's
  parameters in both parameterizations, mean, tail constants and
  `P(X > loc)`, given directly or as the stable limit of a Poisson
  compound with a Pareto tail (#780).
