### Excel

- `PRO.NPV`, `PRO.IRR` and `PRO.MOIC`: returns on flows in cells, at times in years, months or days, or on dates as `XNPV` and `XIRR` read them; one series or a block of simulations, a root beyond the search range named rather than `#NUM!`; Excel's worked examples reproduced (#578).
- `PRO.CASHFLOW`: a collateralised deal's monthly cash flows on simulated loss ratios, its terms a block of labelled rows (every rule of `prospicio_pricing::cashflow`), giving each simulation's IRR, MOIC, NPV and totals, the monthly flows, or one simulation's accounts month by month (#578).
