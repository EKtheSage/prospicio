### Reserving

- `BayesBfNormalised`'s error for a development column whose observed increments sum to zero or less now names the age, its number of observed cells and their sum, says that the chain ladder accepts the triangle (with its factor into that age) and gives the remedies: rebuild the triangle from its long table without the rows at that age, or fold the age into the previous one, and carry the movement left out as a tail. The error for an origin that sums too low names its cells and prints the `cv` below which its posterior is proper. The type docs in Rust, Python and R explain why such a column has no finite mode and how to drop an age (`reserving-bayes.md` decision 26, #610).
