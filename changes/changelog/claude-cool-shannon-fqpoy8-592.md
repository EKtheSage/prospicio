### Aggregate and reinsurance

- A no-claims bonus on a layer: `Layer::no_claims_bonus(share, years, threshold)` returns `share` of the year's premium when the layer's ceded loss over the last `years` contract years is at most `threshold`, beside any sliding scale or profit commission; a tower's result reports it as `no_claims_bonus`, `deal_ratios` as a deduction inside NMBF and `deal_ledger` and the cedant's ledger as a commission leg; `apply_term` and `apply_timeline` look back over the term (the window cut at its start), the grids give `expected_no_claims_bonus` for a one-year bonus and `Quote::technical_for_layer` solves the deposit with it (`aggregate.md`, "No-claims bonus"). Python `Layer.with_no_claims_bonus`, `no_claims_bonus`, `no_claims_bonus_for`; R `with_no_claims_bonus()`, `no_claims_bonus_for()` (#592).
- **Breaking:** `Layer` has a new public field `no_claims_bonus` and both grid results `expected_no_claims_bonus`, so struct literals must add them; a tower with a bonus writes JSON format version 8 (#592).
