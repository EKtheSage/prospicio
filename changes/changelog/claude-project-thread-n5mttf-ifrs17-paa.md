### Reserving

- `ifrs17::paa`: the premium allocation approach. `PaaGroup` takes the
  expected premiums and acquisition cash flows of a coverage and a revenue
  pattern; each period gives insurance revenue and amortisation on the
  pattern (or acquisition expensed when paid), the LIC with its unwind and
  the change for past service (from `Lic` or a reserving fit's
  `DiscountedReserves` and RA), the onerous test's loss component and the
  reconciliation of paragraph 100 (#982).
