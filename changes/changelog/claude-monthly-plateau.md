### Reserving

- Fixed: `DevelopmentPatterns::monthly` with `Monotone::RunningMax` no longer refuses, under the default Weibull reading, a draw that the running maximum holds flat until its last step (nearly complete at its first age, as a bootstrap or Meyers draw that falls after a high first share is held). The fit of the Weibull through such a pattern runs to the edge of its parameters, and the reading now takes the curve at the edge as its prior and still gives the pattern's values exactly; a pattern that falls is still refused. A draw is also read only up to the first age its share reaches 1, with the months after it at 1, so a draw held at 1 from 48 months now needs 48 months of terms (60 on an underwriting basis) instead of 72 (84), and its monthly values under the Weibull reading change; one complete at its first age is read linearly from 0. The same Weibull reading is behind `SelectedPattern::to_underwriting_basis` and the monthly conversions of a selected pattern (Python and R under the default `reading="weibull"`), so a selected pattern that never falls but whose curve fit runs to the edge, which they refused before, now converts there too. No API change (`reserving-v02.md` decision 28 item 4; Python `DevelopmentPatterns.monthly`, R `monthly_pattern_draws()`).
